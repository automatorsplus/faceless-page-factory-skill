# Platform rules the skill enforces or reminds you of

- **Loudness:** -13.8 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP, on every clip. Both platforms play you next to everyone else.
- **Watch it:** frames and audio are two checks. A clip that renders is not a clip that is right.
- **One post per clip.** Reposting the same clip is the fastest way to get a page buried.
- **One door out:** schedule everything through Blotato so metrics land in one place.
- **Lengths:** YouTube Shorts under 60 seconds. TikTok Creator Rewards: over 60 seconds, 10,000 followers,
  100,000 views in the last 30 days.
- **Captions:** TikTok caption plus three to five tags on line one of `caption.txt`; YouTube title under 100
  characters on line two. No double quotes, no em dashes.
- **AI disclosure:** the post bodies mark the clips as AI generated on both platforms. Leave that on.
